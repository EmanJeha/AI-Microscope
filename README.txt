AI MICROSCOPE - SETUP (MIS ICT + Biology)

Files: index.html (that's all).
1. Create a GitHub repository, e.g. AIMicroscope, and upload index.html.
2. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
3. Open https://emanjeha.github.io/AIMicroscope/ in Chrome (on a phone or laptop).

In class: name the classes (e.g. Onion cell, Cheek cell, Leaf cell), add at least 10 examples
to each class (camera over the eyepiece, or upload slide photos), point at a new slide and read
the AI's guess. Press "Check accuracy" for the fair test. "Download training data" saves the work.
Everything runs on the device: no photos are uploaded anywhere.
